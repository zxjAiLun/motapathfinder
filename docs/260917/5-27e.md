## 1. Contract

```ini
TRIGGER =
  feedbackClass == NO_CURRENTLY_EXECUTABLE_LEADING_PREREQUISITE
  AND leading evidence contains unbeatable-at-current-stats OR lethal-at-current-hp

PRIMARY_REUSE   = counterfactual-repair.js
NO              = weighted resource scoring / manual resource thresholds /
                  authored floor milestones / chain length / branch cap / pruning /
                  transport-collapse changes / MT4-before-MT3 /
                  ALREADY_ATTEMPTED retry fix (the 16-branch class is out of scope)
```

Repair experiments are a **formal part of the branch's experiment universe**, not a bypass:

```text
branch experiment universe = normal dependency experiments
                           + conditionally generated resource-repair experiments

normal cannot advance + repair candidate exists -> branch still canAdvance
normal cannot advance + no repair candidate     -> exhausted
```

so the monotonic exhaustion contract from 5.27c/5.27d is preserved.

## 2. Phase 0/1 — Implementation and Synthetic Micro

- `lib/dependency-feedback-controller.js`

    **and** the blocking evidence is a battle-feasibility failure.

## 4. Escalation to 80 Rounds (authorized once)

Conversion > 0 but 40 rounds MISS, so exactly one escalation with **all other controls unchanged**:

| Rounds | Expansions | Max depth | Max cum. decisions | Floors | Conversions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 415 | 13 | 23 | MT1 34 / MT2 29 | 1 |
| 20 | 423 | 21 | 31 | MT1 42 / MT2 37 | 1 |
| 40 | 443 | 41 | 51 | MT1 62 / MT2 57 | 1 |
| 60 | 463 | 61 | 71 | MT1 82 / MT2 77 | 1 |
| **80** | **483** | **81** | **91** | **MT1 102 / MT2 97** | **1** |

**80 rounds still MISS MT3.** The scaling shape is the actual finding:

- Depth and cumulative decisions grow **~1 per round** (depth 41→81, cum 51→91) while expansions stay
  almost flat (443→483; only ~40 more expansions over 40 more rounds).
- Conversions stay frozen at **exactly 1** after round 7.
- Repair attempt breakdown over 80 rounds: 73 selections, of which **70 are `cf-d0-path_unlock`** and
  3 are `cf-d0-exp_level`. From round 12 onward the mechanism repeatedly selects the *same*
  `path_unlock` repair from a chain of MT2 branches, never converting again.

So the lineage is not proliferating — it is **walking one MT2 branch, one decision at a time**, with the
repair mechanism repeatedly picking a repair that does not change the blocked battle evidence.
