## 4. Root Cause of the 4,606 vs 11,604 HP Gap

The trace provides unambiguous, empirical answers to the Reviewer's core questions:

1. **Where was the teacher ancestry lost?**
   - Under exact teacher state identity (`exactTeacherSuccessor`), the teacher lineage is intact through Step 13 (`changeFloor@TS13:1,1`), which was popped and expanded at expansion 6,527.
   - When Step 13 expanded, the exact successor for Step 14 (`battle:slimeman@TS12:9,3`) was **generated, accepted, and enqueued into the priority queue**, but **popped 0 times**.
   - Because exact Step 14 was never popped, exact Step 15+ were **never generated** along this lineage.
   - The 7,149 enqueues recorded for `changeFloor@TS12:6,12` reflect the **action fingerprint** generated across other exploration branches on TS12 that also attempted to return to TS11, all of which were similarly starved at the agenda layer.
2. **Why was it lost?**
   - In `compareDpAgendaRank`:
     ```javascript
     if (left.nextDistance !== right.nextDistance) return right.nextDistance - left.nextDistance;
     ```
   - Under `goal-relative` distance:
     - Nodes on TS13 have distance $d \le 18$.
     - Preparation nodes on TS12 (such as `slimeman@9,3`) have distance $d \ge 30$.
     - Preparation nodes on TS11 have distance $d = 36 \sim 52$.
   - Because `nextDistance` is strictly compared **before** `hp`, nodes with $d \ge 30$ sit permanently at the bottom of the 15,000+ node heap.
   - In 32,000 expansions, **TS11 expansions were strictly ZERO**.
   - Note on 256k production search scope: the 256k production attempt was not instrumented with fine-grained prefix trace, but also resulted in MISS with 55,469 residual frontier states and `actionTrimmed=0`, which is entirely consistent with ongoing service starvation of preparation nodes.
3. **Reconciliation of the 7,000 HP gap**:
   - The 11,604 HP witness relied on Steps 16–20 to harvest the 12,800 HP of potions on TS11.
   - Because autonomous search never popped TS11, it was physically confined to harvesting potions local to TS13 and TS12.
   - Total uncollected potions accessible locally on TS13/TS12 without TS11 is strictly bounded at **~4,606 HP**.
   - **The 7,000 HP gap is 100% accounted for by the queue starvation of TS11 preparation nodes.**
