## 1. Context & Research Objective

PR-5.29a demonstrated that correcting the old-floor `nextDistance=0` anomaly cured service starvation (TS13 service share inverted from 0.35% to 81.38%), yet within an 8k expansion budget, search halted at Manhattan distance 6 (`(8,8)`).

The objective of PR-5.29b is to rigorously determine the nature of this halt:
- **Did the search encounter an inescapable topological cut of insurmountable combat blockers?**
- Or was the frontier at `(8,8)` merely an active, progressing boundary that stopped due to budget exhaustion while possessing legal forward bypasses?

---

## 2. Formal 4-Tier Feasibility Audit Architecture

The audit executes four automated, objective tiers on the best forward frontier states without authored hints or hardcoded level/monster rules:

1. **Tier 1 (Forward Cut Verification)**:
   - Computes the reachable free floor region via flood-fill from the frontier state.
   - Extracts all boundary interactions towards the stage goal.
   - Rigorously tests topological cut connectivity: checks whether every non-wall path to the goal must cross at least one unexecutable blocker, or whether a legal bypass exists.
2. **Tier 2 (Mathematical Deficit Vectors)**:
   - For all boundary battle obstacles, computes:
     - `minAtkForPositiveDamage = enemy.def + 1`
     - `atkDeficit = Math.max(0, minAtkForPositiveDamage - hero.atk)`
     - Exact battle damage & `hpDeficit = Math.max(0, damage + 1 - hero.hp)`
     - Status: `viable-at-current-state`, `lethal-at-current-hp`, or `unbeatable-at-current-stats`.
3. **Tier 3 (Reachable Preparation Capability Envelope)**:
   - Evaluates all reachable pickups (potions, gems) and currently viable monster battles across accessible floors (TS13, TS12, TS11).
   - Computes projected level-ups and theoretical stat upper bounds (`envelopeMaxStats`).
4. **Tier 4 (Four-Way Causal Classification)**:
   - **A: no-forward-cut**: Legal bypasses exist around unexecutable blockers; cut hypothesis is false.
   - **B: resource-feasibility-deficit**: Cut is established and total reachable preparation cannot close the combat gap.
   - **C: preparation-available-but-not-realized**: Preparation exists to close the gap, but search failed to harvest it.
   - **D: capability-realized-but-not-propagated**: Capable states were generated, but search dropped or failed to propagate them to the blocker.

### Detailed Analysis of Findings

1. **Reachable Area Expanded Dramatically**:
   - Starting from `(1,1)` with 4 free tiles, the search under `goal-relative` priority quickly conquered the initial hallway and expanded the reachable free area to **41 ~ 44 tiles**.
   - Hero automatically achieved **Lv 3** (+1 ATK, +1 DEF from level-up) and reached **HP > 4,100, ATK 7, DEF 1**.
2. **Deficit Vector Analysis on Boundary Obstacles**:
   - **`rock@11,7` & `rock@11,3`** (DEF: 8): `unbeatable-at-current-stats` (`minAtkForPositiveDamage = 9`, `atkDeficit = 2`).
   - **`skeletonWarrior@7,7`** (DEF: 3, HP: 80, ATK: 16): `viable-at-current-state` (damage: **285 HP** vs hero HP 4,400 $\to$ remaining HP: 4,115).
   - **`ghostSoldier@6,8`** (DEF: 5, HP: 200, ATK: 30): `viable-at-current-state` (damage: **2900 HP** vs hero HP 4,400 $\to$ remaining HP: 1,500).
   - **`vampire@4,10`** (DEF: 2, HP: 140, ATK: 12): `viable-at-current-state` (damage: **594 HP** vs hero HP 4,400 $\to$ remaining HP: 3,806).
   - **`skeleton@1,9`** (DEF: 6, HP: 150, ATK: 18): `viable-at-current-state` (damage: **2533 HP** vs hero HP 4,400 $\to$ remaining HP: 1,867).
3. **Topological Proof of Legal Bypass**:
   - While the two rocks are unexecutable with ATK 7, they do NOT form a cut.
   - Hero can legally and survivably bypass the rocks via **`skeletonWarrior@7,7` (taking only 285 damage)** or **`ghostSoldier@6,8` (taking 2900 damage)** directly into the TS13 central avenue.
4. **Why did Search Stop at 8,000 Expansions?**:
   - The search did **NOT** hit an unexecutable dead end (`stoppedReason: null`, not `frontier-drained`).
   - At the 8,000 expansion limit, the search had an active, healthy frontier of **2,588 ~ 4,234 pending nodes**.
   - **Halt Root Cause**: Pure expansion budget exhaustion under finite workload (8k), while actively advancing along legal bypass corridors.
