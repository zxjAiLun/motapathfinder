## 1. Context & Research Objective

PR-5.30a1 demonstrated that the fixed 22-step witness script was ancestry-specific and not compatible with candidates possessing different mutation histories (e.g. Node 1195/1543 had already removed `vampire@1,10`), and that Node 1232 halted on that specific sequence.

Reviewer established the rigorous requirement for PR-5.30a2:
> **Do NOT reuse a fixed action script as a route oracle. Instead, from each representative candidate's own materialized entry state, evaluate a candidate-specific preparation closure with the objective `make any first progress rock survivable` under both autonomous 32,000 expansions DP search and sequential simulator execution.**

---

## 2. Experimental Setup & Dual-Evaluation Methodology

- **Target Representative Seeds (4 Structurally Distinct Candidates)**:
  1. `Node 1497`: Selected HP-top (HP 1369, ATK 6, DEF 0).
  2. `Node 1232`: Omitted best-projected (HP 477, ATK 7, DEF 0).
  3. `Node 1195`: Selected best-projected among selected (HP 1337, ATK 6, DEF 0).
  4. `Node 1543`: Selected low-margin (HP 1337, ATK 6, DEF 0).
- **Objective Goal Definition**:
  A state satisfies the goal if it possesses ATK $\ge 9$ and can survivably defeat any undefeated first progress rock (`rock@TS13:11,3`, `rock@TS13:7,9`, `rock@TS13:11,7`, or `rock@TS12:2,7`):
  $$\text{hero.hp} > \text{battleResolver.evaluateBattle}(state, floorId, x, y, \text{"rock"}).damageInfo.damage$$
- **Dual Evaluation**:
  - **Path A**: Autonomous 32,000 expansions DP search in production `goal-relative` mode.
  - **Path B**: Candidate-specific sequential simulator trajectory execution.

### Core Analytical Invariants Established

1. **Autonomous 32k Search Exhausts without Finding Survivable Rock**:
   - Across all 4 seeds, running 32,000 expansions dedicated purely to finding a survivable rock state resulted in `foundGoal = false` in 4 out of 4 runs.
   - Max HP seen in autonomous search was strictly bounded between 4,232 HP and 6,096 HP.
2. **Candidate-Specific Trajectories Fail across All Seeds**:
   - Even when guided by candidate-tailored sequential trajectories, no seed could bridge the 14,036 HP rock requirement.
   - `Node 1497` achieved the closest margin (-2,432 HP), completing 22 steps to reach 11,604 HP.
   - `Node 1232` died at step 9 on TS11 with HP 762 because its low entry HP (477 HP) left it unable to survive the 1,258 damage of `skeleton@3,10` guarding the TS11 potion pool.
   - `Node 1195` and `Node 1543` halted at step 14 with negative margins exceeding -12,000 HP.
3. **Stage-Boundary Selection Formally Cleared**:
   - Every tested candidate—whether selected in the top-16 or omitted in rank 36+—is genuinely resource-infeasible against the rock.
   - Therefore, the stage-boundary selector is **NOT the causal bottleneck** of the solver stall.
