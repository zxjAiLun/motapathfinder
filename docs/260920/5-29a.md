- `(8, 8)` is an open tile (`0`).
- The only forward pathways to `(11, 11)` are blocked by high-defense monsters:

  - `(8, 10) zombie`: HP 400, ATK 21, **DEF 9**.

- Against DEF 9 monsters, ATK $\le$ 8 cannot break defense ($\le 0$ damage $\to$ battle rejected by simulator).
- Breaking DEF 9 requires reaching **Lv 3** (+3 ATK, +3 DEF $\to$ ATK 11), which requires **75 EXP**.
