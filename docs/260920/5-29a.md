## 2. Experimental Results

### A. Service Distribution Inversion (Causal Bottleneck Proven)

| Metric | Control A (Production Agenda) | Arm B (Goal-Relative Agenda) | Gain / Delta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mean TS13 Service Share** | **0.0035 (0.35%)** | **0.8138 (81.38%)** | **233.35x Gain** |
| **Mean Old-Floor Service Share** | **0.9965 (99.65%)** | **0.1862 (18.62%)** | **-81.03% Reduction** |
| **TS11 Exploration** | 7,300 ~ 7,520 pops / entry | **0 pops** (completely eliminated) | **-100% Waste** |
| **TS12 Preparation Exploration**| 400 ~ 620 pops / entry | 1,500 ~ 2,014 pops / entry | **Healthy ~19% Prep** |

- In Control A, the fictitious `nextDistance = 0` assigned to backward entry onto TS12(1,1) caused 99.65% of expansions to be swallowed by old floors (reproducing the long-run starvation observed in cloud 256k).
- In Arm B, goal-relative admissible distance ($d_{TS12 \to TS14} = d_{local} + 20$) eliminated the queue-jumping anomaly:
  - **TS13 forward service expanded from 14~48 pops to 6,000~6,426 pops per entry**.
  - TS12 retained a healthy ~19% service share for viable resource and stat recovery.
  - Irrelevant draining back to TS11 was completely reduced to zero.

#### Physical Bottleneck Analysis at (8, 8):
Even when TS13 forward expansions increased by 233x (expanding 6,400+ nodes in TS13 per entry), search consistently halted at **TS13(8, 8)** (Manhattan distance 6 from the upStair at `(11, 11)`).

Inspection of the TS13 map layout around `(8, 8)` reveals a direct combat feasibility wall:
- `(8, 8)` is an open tile (`0`).
- The directly observed forward pathways toward `(11, 11)` are blocked by high-defense monsters:
  - `(9, 8) skeletonCaptain`: HP 420, ATK 21, DEF 0 (damage: 1092~1449 HP).
  - `(8, 10) zombie`: HP 400, ATK 21, **DEF 9**.
  - `(10, 9) zombieKnight`: HP 333, ATK 18, **DEF 9**.
  - `(7, 7) rock` & `(7, 9) rock`: HP 320, ATK 45, **DEF 8**.
- **Combat Feasibility Deficit**:
  - Un-upgraded preparation routes entering with ATK 6 (and reaching ~8 via TS12 rubies) cannot break DEF 9 (damage $\le 0 \to$ unviable in battle resolver).
  - Reaching Lv 3 (+3 ATK, +3 DEF $\to$ ATK 11) is a strong witness hypothesis to break DEF 9, but whether it forms a true forward cut set requires formal blocker audit (PR-5.29b).
  - No profile quotas, artificial thresholds, or hardcoded level hints will be added to production solver.
