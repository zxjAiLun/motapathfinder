## 1. Context & Research Question

PR-5.29b proved that at the 8k frontier, unexecutable DEF 8/9 monsters do not form an inescapable cut (static bypass exists). However, live cloud long-run evidence demonstrated that even under an expanded **256,000 expansion budget**, production search halted with:
```text
expansions: 256,000
stoppedReason: "expansion-limit"
frontierSize: 66,431
foundGoal: false
maxHeroSeen: { hp: 5639, atk: 9, def: 1, exp: 24 }
```
This directly disproved the simplistic hypothesis that "the only blocker was an 8k budget".

PR-5.29b1 addresses the decisive question:
> **Why did production search fail to convert the viable bypass into a completed TS14 route despite 256k expansions and achieving ATK 9?**

### B. Mathematical Combat Feasibility & Net Resource Margin
From `(8, 8)`, the terminal path to TS14 upFloor `(11, 11)` requires clearing two obstacles:
1. `rock@11,3` [DEF 8, HP 320, ATK 45]:
   - At ATK 9, deals 1 damage/turn, taking **14,036 HP damage**.
   - Defeating `rock@11,3` grants access to `redGem@11,1` (**ATK 9 $\to$ 10**).
2. `zombieKnight@10,9` [DEF 9, HP 333, ATK 18]:

   - Defeating `zombieKnight@10,9` opens immediate, unobstructed access to **`upFloor@11,11` (TS14)**.

#### Total Resource Feasibility Balance:
- **Total damage budget needed**: $14,036 + 11,288 = \mathbf{25,324\text{ HP}}$.
- **Total potion HP available** on TS11-13 (excluding greenDoors): **74,000 HP** (TS11: 15k, TS12: 12.9k, TS13: 46.1k).
- **Net Feasibility Margin**: $74,000 - 25,324 = \mathbf{+48,676\text{ HP}}$!
- **Conclusion**: The bypass is mathematically and physically guaranteed to be survivable with a surplus of over 48,000 HP.

### C. Ancestry & Propagation Attribution: Why 256k Halted
In the 256,000 expansion run, the solver achieved `ATK 9 / DEF 1`, proving that ATK acquisition succeeded. However, it halted with `frontierSize = 66,431` and `maxHpSeen = 5,639`.

The exact causal barrier is identified in `compareDpAgendaRank`:
```javascript
if (left.nextDistance !== right.nextDistance) {
  return right.nextDistance - left.nextDistance;
}
```
1. **Distance vs HP Priority Conflict**:
   - A node standing at `(8, 8)` has `nextDistance = 6`.
   - Any node that detours to collect the necessary 20,000+ HP in potions on TS13 or TS12 has `nextDistance >= 12` (or $\ge 36$).
   - Because `nextDistance` is compared **strictly before** `hp`, the low-HP node ($d = 6, \text{HP} \approx 4,400$) permanently dominates the head of the heap.
2. **Execution Deadlock at Heap Head**:
   - The $d = 6$ nodes at `(8, 8)` are expanded first, but they cannot fight `rock` (damage 14,036 > HP 4,400 $\to$ action rejected as lethal).
   - The high-HP preparation nodes ($\text{HP} \ge 15,000$) that are fully capable of surviving the rock have worse distance ($d \ge 12$), so they are **pushed deep into the heap and remain unserved**.
   - As a result, the frontier balloons to 66,431 unserved nodes while the 256,000 expansion budget is exhausted exploring local low-HP permutations.

## 3. Classification & Architectural Verdict

- **Formal Classification**: **`legal-bypass-enqueued-but-not-served`**.
- The bypass is mathematically viable and sequentially verified.
- The root bottleneck is **not lack of resources, not lack of levels, and not lack of expansions**:
  It is the **unconditioned priority of `nextDistance` over feasibility readiness**, which causes combat-ready high-HP preparation states to be starved in the priority queue behind combat-unready low-distance states.
