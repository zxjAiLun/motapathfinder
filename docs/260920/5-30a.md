## 1. Context & Research Objective

PR-5.29c established that the audited TS13 entry state (`2-0241234...`) had an unclosable 2,187 HP deficit against the 14k rock because its residual uncollected potion pool had been depleted during Stage 1. 

Reviewer established the decisive experimental gate for PR-5.30a:

> - **Outcome A**: 有，而且当前 selector 没选它 $\to$ Stage-boundary selection root cause established $\to$ 下一轮做 generic future-feasibility-aware selector A/B.
> - **Outcome B**: 没有，但 Stage 1 search 产生更优中间 ancestry $\to$ Stage 1 search/goal generation 问题（不要改 selector）.
> - **Outcome C**: 整个 Stage 1 reachable closure 都不存在这样的 seed $\to$ 真正问题继续往 Stage 0 / TS11 追.

## 2. Experimental Setup & Audit Metrics

- **Candidate Corpus**: The complete active goal population of Stage 1 (TS12 $\to$ TS13) captured via `goalArchiveAudit: { captureAllGoalCandidates: true }` (44 active candidates total).
- **Downstream Feasibility Projection per Candidate**:
  1. **Entry Stats**: Current $HP, ATK, DEF, LV, EXP$.
  2. **Unconsumed Potion Pool**: Exact sum of all uncollected potions across TS11, TS12, TS13 outside DEF 8+ blockers.
  3. **Gross HP Pool**: $\text{entryHp} + \text{unconsumedPotionHp}$.
  4. **Minimum Unavoidable Battle Damage**:
     - For ATK 6 entries: 14,649 HP (TS13 Lv3 opening, TS13 skeletons for ATK 8, TS12 slimeman for ATK 9, TS11 skeletons for potions, TS13 skeletonCaptains for potions).
     - For ATK 7 entries: 12,561 HP (saves TS12 slimeman 1,404 HP + reduced damage on bats, vampires, and skeletons).
  5. **Net Achievable HP before Rock**: $\text{grossHpPool} - \text{minUnavoidableBattleDamage}$.
  6. **Downstream Rock Survival Margin**: $\text{netAchievableHpBeforeRock} - 14,036\text{ HP}$.
  7. **Viability Condition**: $\text{survivalMargin} \ge 0$.

### Core Analytical Insights

1. **Selection Bias Inverse to Future Capability**:
   - The candidates that entered TS13 with **ATK 7** (Node 1232 / 5665) had lower immediate HP (477 HP) because they fought `slimeman@9,3` on TS12 to secure the red gem.
   - However, entering TS13 with ATK 7 saves **2,088 HP** in battle damage across TS13 monsters, yielding a **net achievable HP of 17,816 HP** and a **survival margin of +3,780 HP** against the 14k rock.
   - Because `compareGoalStates` prioritizes immediate HP as its absolute first dimension, Node 1232 was relegated to **Rank 36**, and Node 5665 to **Rank 37**.
   - With `candidateLimit = 16`, **the best future capability candidates were 100% discarded**!
2. **Admitted Candidates Possess the Weakest Margins**:
   - The production selector filled slots 0–15 with candidates that entered with high single-point HP (1293–1369 HP) by skipping the TS12 red gem.
   - Those candidates have the **lowest survival margins in the entire population** (+952 to +2,584 HP), leaving them vulnerable to the slightest path deviation or potion timing conflict.
