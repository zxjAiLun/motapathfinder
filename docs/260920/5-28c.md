## 1. Context & Research Questions

Following PR-5.28b (which resolved heap-limit retention via a 256-entry LRU block index cache and proved 256k bounded search without OOM), the investigation evaluated whether stage boundary candidate selection or agenda starvation is the primary bottleneck:
- **Q1**: Does the production lexicographic `HP-first` stage-boundary selector (`goalSkylineLimit = 16`, `compareGoalStates`) systematically discard growth-alternative profiles?
- **Q1b**: Are there omitted candidates that are Pareto-nondominated under documented safe, monotone physical dimensions?
- **Q2**: Does an omitted growth candidate provide genuine downstream continuation capabilities that the selected ATK6 candidates cannot obtain under equal search work?

## 3. Findings

### Q1: Does HP-first top16 discard growth-alternative profiles?
**Verdict**: `YES (ESTABLISHED)`

At Stage 1 goal boundary (`TS13(1,1)`):
- Total active goal candidates generated: **44** (`captureTruncated: false`)
- Profile `atk=6, def=0, lv=2`: **36 candidates** (HP range: 606 ~ 1369) $\to$ **16 selected** (16/36, 44.4% admitted)
- Profile `atk=7, def=0, lv=2`: **8 candidates** (HP range: 410 ~ 477) $\to$ **0 selected** (0/8, 0.0% admitted)

Due to HP-first lexicographic ordering:
- Selected candidates occupied ranks 0 ~ 15 with HP 1293 ~ 1369.
- The entire ATK7 cohort had lower HP (410 ~ 477) because picking up the TS12 ruby required defeating a guard monster.
- **Result**: The ATK7 growth profile was 100% excluded from advancing to Stage 2 by the stage-boundary archive selector.

### Q1b: Are there omitted Pareto-nondominated candidates?
**Verdict**: `YES (ESTABLISHED)`

Under the 6 documented safe physical dimensions (`hp`, `atk`, `def`, `mdef`, `lv`, `exp`):
- **Omitted Pareto-nondominated candidates exist** (8 candidates in total: 6 ATK7 entries with HP 410~477 and 2 ATK6 entries with higher EXP=20).
- Because no selected ATK6 entry has ATK $\ge$ 7, no selected entry can dominate an ATK7 candidate across all safe dimensions.
- Thus, the existence of omitted Pareto-nondominated growth trade-offs is formally established without relying on arbitrary non-monotone quantities or fragile node ID assertions.

### Q2: Downstream Viability & Capability Witness
**Verdict**: `ATK6 RECOVERS GROWTH DOWNSTREAM; AGENDA STARVATION IS PRIMARY BOTTLENECK`

1. **Immediate Legal Survivability at TS13(1,1)**:
   - Initial legal actions: both arms have 4 legal actions, including 2 battle actions (both against `bat`).
   - Both arms can survivably defeat `bat` (damage: 132~332 HP for ATK6, 160~360 HP for ATK7).
   - Neither arm can immediately fight `skeleton (def=6)` or `ghostSoldier (def=5)` because the required damage (2682 HP and 3000 HP respectively) exceeds the ATK7 entries' current HP (410~477 HP).
   - **Finding**: ATK7 stage entry does *not* grant an immediate new survivable battle action at the entry tile.

2. **Downstream Evolution in Search Tree (8,000 expansions probe)**:
   - **Selected ATK6 Arm**:
     - Rapidly recovers `ATK 7` between expansions **97 ~ 405** by exploring TS12 resource paths.
     - Reaches `ATK 8` between expansions **198 ~ 900**.
     - Reaches `DEF 1` between expansions **198 ~ 813**.
   - **Closest Manhattan Distance to TS14 UpStair (11,11)**:
     - Both arms reach a minimum Manhattan distance of **6** (closest coordinates: `(8, 8)`).
     - Neither arm achieves a survivable forward kill on `skeleton` or reaches TS14 within 8,000 expansions.
   - **Service Distribution**:
     - Both arms suffer extreme backward flow: Selected ATK6 has 99.25% ~ 99.85% old-floor expansions; Omitted ATK7 has 98.55% ~ 99.82% old-floor expansions.
     - TS13 activity remains starved (0.15% ~ 1.45%) regardless of whether the entry was ATK6 or ATK7.

### Crucial Architectural Takeaway for Q2
The audit proves that **the selected ATK6 population is not locked out of growth**; within 100~400 expansions it autonomously discovers the TS12 rubies and upgrades to ATK7 and ATK8.

The reason neither ATK6 nor ATK7 can push deeper into TS13 is that **the current agenda comparator assigns distance=0 to the backward floorTransition back to TS12**, causing the search queue to prioritize old-floor exploration by 99%+.

Therefore, **omitted ATK7 candidates do not possess exclusive continuation capability that ATK6 cannot acquire**. Modifying the stage-goal archive to Pareto diversity would not solve the stalled progress because both arms are starved by the agenda.

## 4. Gate Decision

1. **Production solver code**: 100% unchanged.
2. **PR-5.28d (Stage-Goal Pareto Archive A/B)**: **`REJECTED AS LOAD-BEARING FIX / HOLD`**.
   - Q1 and Q1b are established, but Q2 demonstrated that entry growth profile is rapidly recovered by downstream search and is not the primary cause of TS13 stall.
3. **Next Authorized Direction**:
   - Focus directly on **Agenda Starvation & Goal-Relative Priority (PR-5.29)**: de-biasing the comparator so that retreating to old floors does not get a fictitious `distance = 0` priority over forward TS13 exploration.
