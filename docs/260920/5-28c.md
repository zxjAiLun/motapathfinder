## 3. Findings

### Q1: Does HP-first top16 discard growth-alternative profiles?
**Verdict**: `YES (ESTABLISHED)`

At Stage 1 goal boundary (`TS13(1,1)`):
- Total active goal candidates generated: **44**
- Profile `atk=6, def=0, lv=2`: **36 candidates** (HP range: 606 ~ 1369) $\to$ **16 selected** (16/36, 44.4% admitted)
- Profile `atk=7, def=0, lv=2`: **8 candidates** (HP range: 410 ~ 477) $\to$ **0 selected** (0/8, 0.0% admitted)

Due to HP-first lexicographic ordering:
- Selected candidates occupied ranks 0 ~ 15 with HP 1293 ~ 1369.
- The entire ATK7 cohort had lower HP (410 ~ 477) because picking up the TS12 ruby required defeating a guard monster.
- **Result**: The ATK7 growth profile was 100% excluded from advancing to Stage 2.

### Q1b: Are there omitted Pareto-nondominated candidates?
**Verdict**: `YES (ESTABLISHED)`

Among the 28 rejected candidates:
- **4 candidates are strictly Pareto-nondominated** under generic monotone physical dimensions.
- **3 of them are ATK7 growth candidates**:
  - `Node 5665`: HP 477, ATK 7, DEF 0, LV 2, EXP 18, Cleared 65
  - `Node 5752`: HP 460, ATK 7, DEF 0, LV 2, EXP 19, Cleared 68
  - `Node 6013`: HP 410, ATK 7, DEF 0, LV 2, EXP 20, Cleared 69 (Growth advantage vs Top1: ATK +1, EXP +3, Cleared +6)
- **1 candidate is an ATK6 high-exploration candidate**:
  - `Node 6025`: HP 1277, ATK 6, DEF 0, LV 2, EXP 20, Cleared 68 (EXP +3 vs Top1)

### Q2: Downstream Viability Probe (Equal-Work 8k expansions per candidate)
**Verdict**: `QUALITATIVE CAPABILITY CONFIRMED; 8K PREFIX NOT REACHED`

Evaluated across all 16 selected ATK6 candidates and 4 omitted Pareto candidates:

| Dimension | Selected 16 (ATK 6) | Omitted Pareto (ATK 7, e.g. Node 6013) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Immediate Legal / Battle Actions** | 4 legal / 2 battles / 2 survivable | 4 legal / 2 battles / 2 survivable |
| **DEF 6 Skeleton Damage** | $\le 0$ (**Immune / Cannot defeat**) | $7 - 6 = 1$ dmg/turn (**Killable, 2682 HP cost**) |
| **DEF 5 GhostSoldier Damage** | 6,000 HP cost (lethal) | 3,000 HP cost (**50% damage reduction**) |
| **8k Probe TS13 Service Ratio** | Median: **0.0025** (0.25%) | **0.0145** (1.45%, **~6x higher TS13 activity**) |
| **8k Probe Frontier Size** | ~3,100 ~ 4,200 | **7,330** (larger exploratory frontier) |
| **8k Probe TS14 Hit** | 0/16 (`foundGoal: false`) | 0/4 (`foundGoal: false`) |

**Summary for Q2**:
- ATK7 candidates possess mathematically verified combat capabilities (breaking DEF 6 monsters, halving lethal ghost damage) that ATK6 fundamentally lacks.
- Under downstream DP search, ATK7 exhibits 6x higher exploration allocation in TS13 rather than immediately draining backwards.
- However, within an 8k expansion probe budget, neither group reached the distant TS14 stair. Bounded not-found within 8k is evidence, not proof of impossibility.
