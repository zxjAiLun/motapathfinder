### Detailed Analysis of Failure Mechanisms

1. **Why Node 1232 Failed (The Projection False Positive Disproven)**:
   - In PR-5.30a, the arithmetic formula assumed Node 1232 could harvest all 12,800 HP of potions on TS11.
   - However, to harvest those potions on TS11, the hero must defeat `skeleton@TS11:3,10` (DEF 6), which deals **1,258 HP damage** at ATK 8, DEF 1.
   - Because Node 1232 entered TS13 with only **477 HP**, after fighting its way through the TS13 hallway to reach the stairs, it had only **762 HP** when it arrived at TS11!
   - Because $762 < 1258$, the battle against `skeleton@3,10` was **LETHAL** and pruned by the simulator!
   - Node 1232 was completely blocked from harvesting the TS11 potions, halting at **HP 762**!
   - Its actual survival margin is **-13,274 HP**, far from the optimistic arithmetic projection of +3,780 HP!
2. **Why Node 1497 Achieved the Best Actual Result (11,604 HP)**:
   - Because Node 1497 entered with **1,369 HP**, it had enough HP (2,191 HP) when it arrived at TS11 to survive the 1,258 damage of `skeleton@3,10`!
   - It successfully cleared all 4 skeletons on TS11, harvested all 12,800 HP of potions, and reached **11,604 HP**!
   - It achieved the closest actual distance to clearing the rock (-2,432 HP deficit).

## 4. Decision Framework & Gate Verdict

- **Gate Verdict**: **`Case C: projection-optimistic-false-positive-even-omitted-seed-not-survivable`**!
- **Root Cause Ruling**:
  - The arithmetic future-capability projection was an **optimistic false positive** that failed to account for sequential intermediate survival constraints.
  - The stage-boundary selector was **NOT the root cause of the solver stall**; admitting `Node 1232` would not have resulted in progress, because Node 1232 died at HP 762 on TS11.
  - Modifying the stage-boundary selector (`PR-5.30b`) is **FORMALLY ON HOLD / NOT AUTHORIZED**.
- **True Problem Localization**:
  - All candidates reaching TS13 (both selected and omitted) entered with depleted overall resources due to how Stage 0 and Stage 1 were navigated.
  - True resolution must address generating candidates with higher net residual capability through global route optimization across Stage 0 and Stage 1.
