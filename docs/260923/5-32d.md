## Authorization and question

Owner classified PR-5.32c as `NEGATIVE / CLOSED`, stopped `resource-first`, K and comparator-weight tuning, and explicitly authorized this small audit before any feasibility/subgoal design. The question is strictly:

> Did P14 lose future winning capability when the canonical DP skyline rejected it, or did an equivalent/better same-key representative retain that capability?
