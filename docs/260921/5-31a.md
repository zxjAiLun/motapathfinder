## 1. Context & Research Question

PR-5.30a3 established that:
1. Retained TS13 entry `2-7f41` is provably viable and reaches TS14 in 59 strict-replayed decisions.
2. The teacher route requires a temporary detour back to TS12 and TS11 to harvest a red gem (+1 ATK) and potions (+12,800 HP), yielding a net +2,900 HP advantage to survive the DEF 8 rock.
3. Autonomous search under production `goal-relative` ranking severely starves these preparation nodes:
   - Target #7 (`changeFloor@TS13:1,1`, $d=20$) is enqueued at expansion 8 but starved for 6,639 expansions.
   - Target #8 (`battle:slimeman@TS12:9,3`, $d=29$) is enqueued but never popped within 8k, 16k, or 32k expansions, because $d=29$ is strictly worse than any frontier node on TS13 ($d \le 18$).
   - Floor service on TS11 is strictly 0.0%.

Reviewer authorized **PR-5.31a Bounded Agenda Fairness A/B**:
> **Can a bounded aging / fairness lane (1 fair pop every $N$ best-first pops) dissolve the detour starvation bottleneck and recover the teacher prefix without sacrificing goal-directed search efficiency?**

---

## 2. Experimental Setup & Arms

- **Seed State**: `2-7f41f60b09a55951abd8931c` (HP 1353, ATK 6, DEF 0, Lv 2, EXP 18).
- **Budget**: 8,000 expansions.
- **Arms**:
  - **Control**: Production `dpAgendaMode: "best-first"` (Goal-Relative).
  - **Treatment A**: `dpAgendaMode: "hybrid-fair"`, `fairnessEvery: 32` (1 oldest active node popped every 32 best-first pops).
  - **Treatment B**: `dpAgendaMode: "hybrid-fair"`, `fairnessEvery: 16` (1 oldest active node popped every 16 best-first pops).
- **Active Safety Invariant**:
  The fairness lane strictly verifies `isActiveEntry(entry)` and `!expandedNodeIds.has(entry.nodeId)`, guaranteeing that dominated, superseded, or already-expanded states are skipped and never re-serviced.

## 4. Key Findings & Causal Attribution

1. **Service Debt Radically Reduced**:
   - In Control, P7 (`changeFloor@TS13:1,1`) sat in the queue for **6,639 expansions** before being popped at 6,647.
   - In Treatment B, P7 was popped at **expansion 415 (wait: 407 expansions)**. This is a **16.3x acceleration**, bringing the detour decision into the very opening phase of search.
2. **P8 Detour Deadlock Broken**:
   - In Control, P8 has $d=29$ and was completely starved (popped 0 times in 8k, 16k, and 32k).
   - In Treatment B, P8 was successfully popped at **expansion 7,935**, advancing the exact continuous teacher prefix from Step 7 to Step 8 within the 8k budget!
3. **TS11 Floor Service Activated**:
   - Control strictly gave 0 expansions to TS11.
   - Treatment B gave 15 expansions to TS11, proving that preparation paths on lower floors are no longer permanently locked out.
4. **Safety Contract Empirically Validated**:
   - Treatment B skipped 304 inactive/dominated states and 416 already-expanded states during fairness pops, proving that the fairness lane maintains DP dominance integrity and does not resurrect dead nodes.
