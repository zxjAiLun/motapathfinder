## Results

Structural confirmation first: `battle:skeletonWarrior@MT1:2,1` is decision **#8** in all three tracked fixtures (MT2: 23 decisions, MT3: 33, MT4: 55) - the "cp#8" position is not an MT4 coincidence.

### Phase 1 - cp#8 immediate delta: identical across all three routes

```ini
HP    = 1127 -> 775    (cost: -352, the battle's damage estimate)
ATK   =   10 -> 15     (investment gained: +5 ATK)
EXP   =    1 -> 2      (+1)
HATRED =  28 -> 30
FLOOR_MUTATIONS = the (2,1) skeletonWarrior tile removed
INVENTORY / EQUIPMENT / LV / MONEY = no change
```

The battle buys an ATTACK investment at an HP cost. It is not a transport unlock and carries no items.

### Phase 2 - deletion counterfactual: all three suffixes FAIL at step 10


### Phase 3 - the observed causal chain (simulator facts only)

Probe world after step 9 vs the oracle's pre-state at step 10:

```ini
PROBE  = hp 1236, atk 12, def 8, exp 2,  lv 3
```

```text

   reward scales with hero state (probe +2 vs oracle +4)

   probe hero (hp 1,236 < damage)

   the deficit breaks the run exactly at the MT1 exit preparation
```

No guessing: every link above is a simulator-observed state or enumeration fact.

### Phase 4 - cross-route recurrence: confirmed

MT2, MT3 and MT4 show the SAME cp index, the SAME scalar deltas, and the SAME failure at the SAME step with the SAME mechanism. This is a RECURRING optional-resource-preparation dependency across three independent tracked solutions, not an MT4-witness accident.
