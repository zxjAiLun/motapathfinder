### Phase 1 - cp#8 immediate delta: identical across all three routes


The battle buys an ATTACK investment at an HP cost. On this counterfactual it carries no inventory/equipment change; the transport question is isolated separately in Repair 1 below (the step-10 target tile remains reachable without cp#8).
