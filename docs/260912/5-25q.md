The bug, confirmed from Diagnostic 1 output (`routes/generated/pr525p-frontier-differential.json`, pre-repair):

- `actionToSemanticIdentity()` extracts coordinates once via `action.target || action.stance`.
- Battle and openDoor enumerated actions carry `target: {x,y}` (the battle/door tile), so their identities are correct and genuinely guided (winner legal successors: battle 71/98 BOTH_GUIDED).
- Enumerated changeFloor actions carry NO `target` (`simulator.js` changeFloor builder: `stance` = hero position, `x/y` = stair tile). Their identity therefore used the hero stance tile, e.g. `changeFloor:MT2:6,11->MT3` where the frontier POI is `changeFloor:MT2:6,12->MT3`. Measured: `enumeratedChangeFloorWithTarget = 0`, winner changeFloor steps 6/6 NEITHER, legal successors 23/23 NEITHER. All changeFloor frontier guidance was inert since introduction.

## Primary fix (frozen)

Only inside the `changeFloor` branch of `actionToSemanticIdentity()`, coordinates resolve:

```text
explicit action.target.x/y
else action.x/y            (the stair tile - this is what current primitive actions hit)
else action.stance.x/y     (fallback)
```

The shared `const target = action.target || action.stance` extraction above the branch is intentionally NOT touched: battle, openDoor, pickup/interactPickup, event and the generic fallback keep byte-for-byte identical behavior.

Interpretation boundaries:

- The repair is mechanistically verified live: guided changeFloor children were generated, retained, and expanded (1,844 / 1,822), and forward-floor guided transitions occurred (4). The previously dead guidance now participates in admission.
- The requalification did NOT reproduce the PR-5.25o capability under the same frozen budget in this one run: `MT3_CAPABILITY_AFTER_REPAIR = NOT_OBSERVED_UNDER_FROZEN_CAP_1024_THIS_RUN`. Per the predeclared gate `IF_MT3_FAIL = STOP_AND_RETURN`, no MT4 run, no rerun, and no tuning was performed.
- This does not invalidate the PR-5.25o winner: the promoted artifact, its route, and its strict replay remain valid historical results obtained under the pre-repair mechanism.
- Expansion throughput in this run (66.3/s over the full 180s) is consistent with prior late-run levels on this machine (PR-5.25p MT4 run 71.8/s over 180s; cap-256 attempt 56.9/s over 75.6s). The PR-5.25o winner run's higher average (111/s over 91.6s) reflects reaching the goal before the late phase. There is no evidence the repair slowed per-expansion cost; what changed is the search trajectory - corridor changeFloor transitions now take rank-10 guided admission that they never had before, reshaping which candidates survive cap=1024 and in what order the budget is spent.
- `CAUSE_OF_MT3_REQUALIFICATION_MISS = UNRESOLVED`. The honest statement is: with corrected changeFloor identity, this frozen configuration did not find MT3 in one run; whether trajectory reshaping, budget placement, or run-to-run variance dominates is not established by a single run.
